THE SACRED GEOMETRY OF OUR NAME

The philosophy of
Alif, Laam, Meem.

Our house takes its name from the mystical letters of the Arabic script — representing the foundational principles that guide every sketch, stone, and gold alloy we touch.

ا

Alif • The Root

The upright, singular stroke. It stands for purity of raw material — 100% hallmarked 21K and 22K gold, unadulterated and true to weight from the moment it enters our furnace.

ل

Laam • The Flow

The graceful, curved arc. It embodies the fluidity of design — how a choker wraps around the neck, how a bangle rests against the pulse, how light flows through hand-cut stones.

م

Meem • The Legacy

The closed circle of completion. It represents enduring permanence — crafting pieces built to survive decades of wear and be passed down to daughters and granddaughters.

ROOTS IN LAHORE

Historic craft.
Modern feeling.

Founded in the cultural heart of Lahore, our atelier was nurtured by the rich architectural legacy of Mughal domes, intricate jali lattice work, and the royal Nizami jewel courts. We keep the handmade character alive while creating silhouettes that feel effortless for contemporary life.

FOUR DECADES OF DEVOTION

The milestones of our house.

From a small gold-carving bench in Old Lahore to a premier fine jewellery house with clients across the globe.

1984

The First Karigar Bench

Our founding master goldsmith establishes a bespoke jewellery workshop in Lahore, hand-crafting 22K solid gold bridal sets for families across Punjab.

1998

Revival of Kundan & Jadau

Alif Laam Meem introduces its signature 24K gold-foil kundan technique, reviving antique Mughal jewel-setting for a new generation of brides.

2012

The Purity Charter

We institute full certified assay hallmarking and transparent live-rate gold pricing, setting a benchmark for honest client relationships in Pakistan.

2020

The Flagship Lahore Atelier

Opening of our flagship boutique and private viewing lounge at Al Rehman Square Mall, Sharaqpur, offering personalized 1:1 bridal consultations.

2026

Dilkash 2026 & Global Concierge

Unveiling the Dilkash Bridal 2026 suite alongside our AI Voice Concierge, bringing Lahore’s master jewellery craft to clients worldwide with insured doorstep delivery.

WHAT WE PROMISE

The four sacred pillars
of our atelier.

Every piece that bears the Alif Laam Meem hallmark is bound by four non-negotiable standards of trust, craft, and care.

✦

Certified 21K Purity

Every piece is crafted in BIS-standard 21K or 22K pure gold. We never compromise on metal density or alloy honesty, tested and certified for lifelong value.

◇

Conflict-Free Diamonds

Every diamond and precious gemstone is ethically sourced, natural, and individually inspected under high-magnification for brilliance and cut precision.

◎

Living Karigar Heritage
