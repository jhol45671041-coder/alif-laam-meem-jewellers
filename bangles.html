WRIST MASTERCLASS

Bangle sizing & diameter guide.

South Asian bangles are measured by internal diameter in inches and fraction eighths (anna scale).

2.4

Size 2.4 (Small)

Internal diameter 2.25 inches (57.2 mm). Ideal for petite wrists or closer-fitting solitary bangles.

2.6

Size 2.6 (Medium)

Internal diameter 2.375 inches (60.3 mm). The most popular standard size for bridal sets and everyday bangles.

2.8

Size 2.8 (Large)

Internal diameter 2.50 inches (63.5 mm). Provides comfortable movement over the hand knuckles.
