A PRIVATE, COLLABORATIVE JOURNEY

Four steps to your piece.

You do not need a finished technical sketch. A photograph, a family heirloom, or a single word is enough to begin.

01

Listen & Consult

We discover what the piece should hold, your aesthetic preferences, metal purity choice, and how you plan to wear it.

02

Sketch & 3D Render

Our design team creates full 3D photorealistic digital models and wax mockups so you can approve proportions and stone settings.

03

Karigari Handcraft

Master goldsmiths, Jadau setters, and Meenakari artisans bring the piece to life in certified 21K gold over 2 to 4 weeks.

04

Hallmark & Deliver

Final polishing, assay purity hallmarking, luxury velvet presentation box, and insured nationwide doorstep delivery.
