FREQUENTLY ASKED

Questions are
welcome.

Still wondering? Send us a message and we will answer with the same care we put into the jewellery.

How is my jewellery priced?

Every piece is priced by its net gold weight at the day’s 21K rate, with stone and workmanship details included in your quote.

Can I visit before I decide?

Yes. Book a private viewing in our Lahore showroom and we will prepare a selection around your taste, occasion and budget.

Can you resize a ring?

We can advise on resizing depending on the design. If you are unsure, select “Need help choosing” on a ring’s page or message our team before ordering.

How should I care for my gold?

Keep it in its pouch, away from moisture, perfume and harsh chemicals. Remove jewellery before bathing, swimming or exercising, and bring it to us for professional care when needed.
