ATELIER PRESERVATION GUIDE

How to care for your jewellery.

Simple daily habits ensure your gold, diamonds, and hand-strung pearls maintain their radiant lustre forever.

✦

Solid 21K Gold Care

Gold can be cleaned with lukewarm water and a drop of mild pH-neutral soap using a soft baby toothbrush. Pat dry with a microfiber cloth. Avoid chlorinated swimming pools.

◇

Polki & Kundan Preservation

Kundan and uncut polki pieces are set with organic foil and lac. Never submerge in water. Wipe gently with a dry cotton cloth and store wrapped in muslin inside airtight boxes.

◎

Certified Natural Diamonds

Diamonds attract natural body oils and lotions. Soak in warm soapy water for 10 minutes once a month to restore full spectral fire and brilliance.

◌

Natural Basra & Seed Pearls

“Last on, first off.” Put your pearl jewellery on after perfumes, hairsprays, and cosmetics have fully dried. Wipe with a damp cloth after each wear.
