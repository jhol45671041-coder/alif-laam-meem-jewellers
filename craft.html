THE SIX PILLARS OF KARIGARI

The master techniques of our house.

Every piece at Alif Laam Meem requires up to 40 hours of precision handwork across specialized artisan benches.

01

The Art of Kundan

Layers of hyper-refined 24K pure gold foil are pressed cold with specialized agate burnishers around each stone, creating an airtight, luminous golden bezel that never loosens.

02

Jadau Stone Setting

Natural uncut polki diamonds are individually bedded in pure gold leaf. The natural facets catch ambient candlelight with organic, soft warmth unmatched by modern machine cuts.

03

Meenakari Enamelling

The reverse side of our royal pieces is engraved with floral reliefs and filled with powdered mineral pigments, kiln-fired at 850°C for rich ruby, emerald, and cobalt hues.

04

Tarakashi Filigree

Gold wire drawn as thin as silk thread is hand-twisted and soldered into airy lace lattices, creating feather-light statement earrings and delicate choker borders.

05

21K Hallmarked Refining

We blend 87.5% pure gold with copper and silver alloys to achieve the signature warm ALM tone — offering perfect durability for daily wear while retaining bullion value.

06

Micro-Pavé Diamond Setting

Certified diamonds are aligned under 40x optical magnification with minimal metal prongs, allowing maximum light refraction and seamless, snag-free surfaces.
