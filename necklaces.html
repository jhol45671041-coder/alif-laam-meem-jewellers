STYLING MASTERCLASS

The necklace length guide.

Choosing the right proportion ensures your necklace sits harmoniously against different necklines and bridal ensembles.

14–16"

Collar & Choker

Rests high against the base of the throat. Ideal for sweetheart, boat neck, and deep bridal necklines (e.g., Meher Kundan & Dilruba Choker).

18–20"

Princess & Matinee

Rests gracefully over the collarbone. The classic versatile length for everyday pendants and medallion talismans (e.g., Amaan & Aayat).

28–34"

Royal Long Haar & Opera

Falls generously below the bustline. Creates majestic royal drama when layered over a choker for bridal ceremonies (e.g., Firdaus Haar).
