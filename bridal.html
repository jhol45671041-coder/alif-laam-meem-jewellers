THE CEREMONY OF JEWELLERY

The 5 bridal rituals & how to layer.

From the sunlit turmeric glow of the Mayun to the regal grandeur of the Barat — explore how our bridal suites transition across your wedding festivities.

01

The Mayun & Haldi

Delicate seed-pearl drops, lightweight 21K floral studs, and fine gold bangles that celebrate natural grace and warmth.

02

The Vibrant Mehndi

Joyful Navratna stones, Meenakari enamelled jhumkas, and fluid haathphools made to catch the candlelight on hennaed hands.

03

The Royal Barat

The Dilkash Choker paired with the Firdaus Long Haar, Kundan Mathapatti, and Nath for an unforgettable entrance.

04

The Sacred Nikkah

Luminous Basra pearls, 21K antique gold collar, and single-stone solitaire rings that honor the sanctity of your vows.

05

The Regal Walima

Modern uncut diamond polki sets with Zambian emerald accents and sculpted cuffs for a refined, luminous reception.

Every ALM bridal jewel is engineered with detachable drops and adjustable dori cords, allowing you to re-wear each piece for years.
